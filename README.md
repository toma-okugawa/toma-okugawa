## About

I grew up on Yuge, a small island in Kamijima Town, Ehime, and now study at the National Institute of Technology, Yuge College (KOSEN). My research focuses on low-light image enhancement (Retinex) and real-time human pose estimation, with side projects in small language models, regional policy, and entrepreneurship education. I turn research into ventures — **Kyudo AI** and **Beacon** — make generative art, and learn in public, including from failure.

## Featured

### [SD-Retinex](https://github.com/toma-okugawa/sd-retinex)

A training-free, CPU-oriented low-light method: a stable real-axis kernel (the sigmoid derivative) and a bounded sigmoid tone map.
