## About

I am a student researcher at the **National Institute of Technology, Yuge College**. My work centers on perceptual information processing—particularly low-light image enhancement, Retinex theory, illumination estimation, computer vision, and real-time human pose estimation.

I also explore intelligent informatics and its social applications, including explainable AI, small language models, retrieval-augmented generation, regional policy, and smart cities. A parallel strand of my work examines entrepreneurship and learning in KOSEN education.

## Featured work

### [SD-Retinex](https://github.com/toma-okugawa/sd-retinex)

A training-free, CPU-oriented method for low-light image enhancement. SD-Retinex combines a stable real-axis sigmoid-derivative illumination kernel with a bounded tone map, preserving the analytical Retinex structure while avoiding the pole-driven instability of the preceding XCR formulation.
